Pizza

Pizza (Italian: ['pittsa'],Neapolitan: ['pittse']) is a savoury dish of Italian origin consisting of a usually round,flattened base of leavened wheat-bread dough topped with tomatoes, cheese, and often varius other ingredients(such anchovies, mushrooms, onions, olive, pineapple,meat, etc.), which is then baked at a high temperature,trditionally in a wood-fired oven.[1] A small pizza is sometimes called a pizzeta. A person who makes pizza is known as a pizzaiolo.
